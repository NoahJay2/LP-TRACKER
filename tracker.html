How syncing works
  • Stock — the tracker owns the count. Website orders deduct here once, and every change to a linked product updates the store.
  • Orders — website orders mirror their WooCommerce status: Pending payment and On-hold show as unpaid, Processing → Paid, Completed → Paid + Fulfilled. WooCommerce sends its own payment emails and cancels unpaid orders; cancelled, failed or refunded web orders are removed and their stock returned. Orders placed before the store was connected are never imported, and a web order matching one you already entered (same customer, day and items) is skipped.
  • Products — images and SKUs come from the store. Tracker products you don't link stay private.

Product links

Choose which store product each tracker product matches. Linking pushes the tracker's quantity to the store. Products set to “Don't sync” are left alone on both sides.
